## Brand & Style

The brand personality is welcoming, sophisticated, and devoted to premium companion care. Situated in the lush, residential and diplomatic enclave of Clayton, Panama, the identity bridges neighborhood warmth with refined boutique veterinary wellness and grooming spa services. The digital presence must convey supreme hygiene, gentle handling, and artisanal pet lifestyle curation.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Warmth** with subtle **Glassmorphism** and tactile touches:
- Generous, airy negative space grounded by velvety royal purples and fresh botanic lime accents that honor the physical storefront facade and brand legacy.
- High-end boutique layout that avoids chaotic retail clutter in favor of tranquil service showcases (spa bathing, feline quiet grooming, veterinary nutrition consultation, and swift local courier delivery across Panama City).
- A playful yet elevated spirit: professional typography and curved containment cards celebrate the unconditional bond between pets and pet parents without feeling cartoonish or juvenile.

## Colors

The color palette translates the physical purple facade and high-energy lime accents into an elevated, editorial digital experience.

- **Primary Violet (`#5D2E8C`)**: Serves as the authoritative foundation for core brand signifiers, headers, high-emphasis action states, and key navigational milestones.
- **Secondary Lime Vitality (`#52C41A` / `#7ED957`)**: Used intentionally for lively accents—appointment tags, instant booking badges, active status indicators, and high-conversion call-to-action highlights. It injects vitality and freshness into wellness and grooming sections.
- **Tertiary Amethyst (`#7B3FA8`)**: Provides smooth chromatic gradients, interactive hover states, and layered container cards.
- **Warm Neutral & Canvas (`#F9F8FC` & `#FFFFFF`)**: Ensures maximum optical comfort, pristine cleanliness, and high contrast against rich jewel tones.
- **Deep Neutral Ink (`#1E1627`)**: A tinted plum-charcoal tailored for crystal-clear readability without the harshness of pure black.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans brings a contemporary, welcoming geometry with subtly softened terminals that mirror friendly paws and pristine pet wellness. 

- **Display & Large Headlines**: Leverage tighter kerning (`-0.02em`) and bold weights to evoke modern editorial flair for salon offerings, boutique retail collections, and Panama home delivery promises.
- **Body Hierarchy**: Uses open apertures and generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) for effortless skimming of ingredient lists, booking time slots, and grooming service packages.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Employ higher weights (`600` and `700`) with uppercase or title casing for quick category recognition (e.g., "GROOMING SPA", "BOUTIQUE DIETS", "CLAYTON HUB").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max page constraint of `1280px` centered with `2.5rem` horizontal margins, `1.5rem` gutters, and expansive `5rem` to `6rem` vertical padding between distinct retail and salon sections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Cards reflow into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single column with `1rem` edge margin and a unified `0.75rem` vertical spacing rhythm. Booking actions and store contact triggers stick to a bottom floating action dock.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`) dictate component padding, badge spacing, and category filter chips to maintain an orderly and tactile cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous layered surfaces and violet-tinted atmospheric diffusion rather than heavy dark shadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Warm pearl white canvas (`#FDFCFE`) with subtle perimeter vignettes.
- **Card Surfaces (Level 1)**: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate purple-tinted boundary outline (`rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.08)`) and ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.06)`.
- **Interactive Elevated Containers (Level 2 - Hover & Featured)**: Subtle upward translation (`-3px`) paired with enriched soft diffusion: `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Modals & Quick Booking Drawers (Level 3)**: Supported by a frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `rgba(30, 22, 39, 0.45)`, providing clean isolation for appointment scheduling and shopping basket reviews.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, organic radii (`roundedness: 2`, `8px` base, `16px` for standard cards, and `24px` for featured promotional modules). 

- Buttons and micro-badges adopt full pill outlines (`rounded-full`) to communicate approachability, warmth, and ease of interaction.
- Product photography and grooming portfolio cards utilize balanced `16px` to `20px` corner radii, complementing the friendly visual DNA of companion animals without feeling unstructured.
- Modal panels and bottom sheets feature generous top radii (`24px`) that guide the user’s gaze gracefully into form inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Grooming / Order)**: Solid deep violet (`#5D2E8C`) with crisp white label, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover expands into rich amethyst (`#7B3FA8`) with subtle elevation lift.
- **Secondary Accent (VIP / Delivery / WhatsApp)**: Vivid lime (`#52C41A`) with deep violet typography (`#27123D`) for immediate visual pop on high-priority interactions.
- **Ghost / Outline**: 1.5px border in violet tint (`rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.25)`), transitioning to filled subtle tint (`rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.05)`) on hover.

### Product & Service Cards
- **Grooming Spa Card**: Clean white container with top photography showcase, followed by category tag in light mint pill, concise package specs (bath, haircut, ear cleaning), pricing in bold violet, and an instant date reservation CTA.
- **Boutique Retail Card**: Clean image canvas with subtle neutral backdrop (`#F7F5FA`), subtle floating favorite heart icon, clear brand typography, and an "Añadir al Carrito" pill button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Soft pearl-tinted background (`#F9F8FC`), `1.5px` border in soft neutral-gray (`rgba(93, 46, 140, 0.12)`), `12px` rounded corners. Focus state yields an energetic lime ring (`#52C41A`, 2px) and white interior.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Smooth rounded squares and circles filled with primary violet (`#5D2E8C`) and crisp white checks when active.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Horizontally scrollable category pills ("Perros", "Gatos", "Spa & Corte", "Alimento Holístico", "Accesorios"). Selected states feature solid `#5D2E8C` with white typography; unselected states feature subtle neutral backgrounds with soft purple text.

### Badges & Delivery Status
- Micro badges displaying "Clayton Delivery Express" and "Grooming Certificado" use a dual-tone style: 10% lime tint background with `#388E0C` text, paired with rounded-full geometry.
